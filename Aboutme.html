About Me

Sonrisa Watts is a behavioral researcher and artificial intelligence strategist from St. Kitts and Nevis.

Born and raised on the twin-island nation—a community of just 48,000 people—Sonrisa built Emotion Encoded entirely from the ground up. With no institutional backing, no prior mentors, and no traditional network, she relied on little more than a laptop, relentless grit, and a clear vision.

At age 24, she founded Emotion Encoded as an independent research initiative investigating cognitive-emotional barriers to AI adoption in high-stakes industries.

Operating completely autonomously, she executed extensive mixed-methods studies and conducted professional interviews across a diverse and elite cohort, including surgeons, anesthesiologists, oncologists, radiologists, cardiologists, founders of telehealth and behavioral science startups, physicians in internal medicine, attorneys-at-law, a biomedical engineering professor of Johns Hopkins University, and psychologists. Eight months later, her proprietary cross-sector datasets captured global attention.

This breakthrough work culminated in a formal invitation from the Stanford University School of Medicine to integrate her findings into their Global AI Implementation Curriculum and first-author a landmark clinical AI implementation manuscript.
